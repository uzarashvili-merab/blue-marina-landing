Privacy Policy

Draft for review by Housebook's legal team before launch.

This website is operated by Housebook Real Estate L.L.C ("Housebook", "we") to present off-plan residences on Dubai Islands.

What we collect

Your name, phone or WhatsApp number, Telegram username (if you choose Telegram), your answers to the questionnaire (budget, unit type, purpose, payment preference, timing), and technical data such as IP address, browser type and advertising identifiers (Meta cookies, UTM parameters).

Why we collect it

To send you the shortlist and payment schedule you requested, to contact you about the properties you are interested in via WhatsApp, Telegram or phone, to verify that the number you provided is reachable on WhatsApp, and to measure and improve our advertising.

Who we share it with

Our sales team and, where needed to answer your request, the property developer; service providers that host our website, store leads and deliver messages; and Meta Platforms for advertising measurement (in hashed form). We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop contacting you, at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or Telegram or writing to us at the contact address below.
